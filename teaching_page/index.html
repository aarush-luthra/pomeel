Dark Patterns in Online Shopping

Six interface tricks named in India's CCPA Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023, each shown next to an honest version of the same screen. The examples are interactive: try the buttons. Every store name here is invented.

False Urgency

CCPA 2023 · OECD: Urgency, Social proof

Claiming or implying that time or stock is running out, or that many others are buying, when it isn't true, so you buy before you think.

Manipulative
Kalpa Audio kalpa-audio.example
Kalpa Buds Pro · Wireless Earbuds
₹1,999
Only 2 left in stock!
37 people are viewing this right now
Deal ends in 00:09:59

The tell: reload and the clock starts again at ten minutes, and the stock is still "2". The deadline was never real.

Honest
Kalpa Audio kalpa-audio.example
Kalpa Buds Pro · Wireless Earbuds
₹1,999
In stock · ships in 2 days
Festive sale price valid until 3 Nov, 11:59 pm IST.

Why it's fair: the sale has a fixed calendar end date that is the same for everyone and every visit.

Why it works

Scarcity and loss aversion. When we think we might lose a deal, we weigh the loss more heavily than the gain and skip comparing prices.

Drip Pricing

CCPA 2023 · OECD: Sneaking

Showing a low price first and revealing the other parts of the price (platform, handling or convenience fees) only at the cart or checkout.

Manipulative
Gulmohar Beauty Step 1 of 4
Saffron Glow Face Serum, 30 ml
Item price₹499
To pay₹499

The tell: each step adds a fee you could not see on the product page. By the time you notice, you've already committed.

Honest
Gulmohar Beauty Product page
Saffron Glow Face Serum, 30 ml
₹596
All-inclusive price. Includes delivery, packaging and all taxes. The amount you see is the amount you pay.
To pay at checkout₹596

Why it's fair: one total, shown up front, and it doesn't change at checkout.

Why it works

Anchoring and sunk effort. The first price sets your reference point, and after several checkout steps backing out feels like wasted effort.

Basket Sneaking

CCPA 2023 · OECD: Sneaking

Adding products, services, donations or charges to your cart without your clear consent, often as boxes that are already ticked.

Manipulative
Kalpa Audio Your cart
Kalpa Buds Pro₹1,999
Total₹2,158

The tell: you never chose these extras. They are in the total unless you notice and untick them.

Honest
Kalpa Audio Your cart
Kalpa Buds Pro₹1,999
Total₹1,999

Why it's fair: extras are offered, not assumed. You opt in.

Why it works

Default bias. Most people leave defaults as they are, so a pre-ticked box turns inattention into a sale.

Confirm Shaming

CCPA 2023 · OECD: Interface interference

Wording the "no" option to make you feel guilty, foolish or ashamed for declining.

Manipulative
Thread & Loom Pop-up

Wait! Take 10% off your first order

Join our list for members-only prices.

The tell: the decline link insults you, and it's styled small and grey so it's easy to miss.

Honest
Thread & Loom Pop-up

Take 10% off your first order

Join our email list. Unsubscribe any time.

Why it's fair: both choices are neutral, visible and equally easy to click.

Why it works

Self-image. Clicking a button that says you "like paying full price" feels like admitting to something, so people click yes to avoid it.

Subscription Trap

CCPA 2023 · OECD: Obstruction, Sneaking

Making it easy to sign up and hard to cancel, hiding auto-renewal terms, or taking payment details for a "free" trial without saying what happens next.

Manipulative
Gulmohar Beauty Glow Club
Try Glow Club FREE
Free delivery and early access to sales.
Card required. After 7 days membership renews automatically at ₹299/month until cancelled. To cancel, call customer care between 10 am and 5 pm on working days.

The tell: the price and the auto-renewal sit in tiny grey text, and cancelling needs a phone call during office hours.

Honest
Gulmohar Beauty Glow Club
Glow Club · 7 days free, then ₹299/month
We'll remind you by email 2 days before the trial ends. Cancel in one tap from Account → Membership, any time, and you won't be charged.

Why it's fair: the real price is in the headline, you get a reminder, and cancelling takes as long as joining.

Why it works

Present bias and inertia. "Free" today feels concrete, the future charge feels distant, and a hard cancellation process means many people never get round to it.

Inflated Reference Price

CCPA 2023: Interface interference · OECD: Interface interference

Crossing out a "regular" price that was never really charged, so an ordinary price looks like a huge discount.

Manipulative
Thread & Loom thread-loom.example
Indigo Linen Kurta
₹999₹4,99980% OFF
Price over the last 90 days
₹999 every day"was" ₹4,999: never charged

The tell: the product has always sold at ₹999. The ₹4,999 exists only to make the price look like a bargain.

Honest
Thread & Loom thread-loom.example
Indigo Linen Kurta
₹999₹1,29923% off
Price over the last 90 days
Sold at ₹1,299 until last weeknow ₹999

Why it's fair: the crossed-out price is one customers actually paid, and the % off matches the two numbers.

Why it works

Anchoring. The struck-through number becomes the reference point, so ₹999 feels like saving ₹4,000 rather than paying ₹999.
